## Other solutions

People I know had come up with a variety of creative ways to more easily access their timetable, including but not limited to:
- Starring the PNG on WhatsApp (most common)
- The class representative putting it as the group DP
- Using the Nothing OS image widget to put it on their home screen
- Using it as the home screen wallpaper 
- A custom AutoHotkey script to open the image file on Windows
